Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Who uses the app

WhereHouse Publisher is used only by WhereHouse to manage its own social media accounts. It does not collect data from members of the public.

What data it handles

When WhereHouse connects its own TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the account's display name and ID) from TikTok. This is used only to upload WhereHouse's videos to that account.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on WhereHouse's own computer and are not shared, sold or sent to any third party. They can be revoked at any time from the connected account's settings.

Deletion

Disconnecting the app in TikTok settings revokes access, and WhereHouse deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions: message @wherehousecollective on Instagram or TikTok.
